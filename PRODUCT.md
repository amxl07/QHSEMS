## Users

Primary: corporate and project decision-makers who buy HSE capability — HSE managers and directors, project/construction managers, plant and operations managers, contractor HSE leads, and procurement/tender teams at petrochemical, oil & gas, heavy chemical and other manufacturing, construction, commissioning, energy, warehousing and healthcare organisations. Their situation: a live project, an audit finding, a tender requirement, a regulator or client HSE clause, or an incident — they need a competent partner who can produce the system, the documentation and the trained people, and who can start on site.

Secondary (browse, do not convert): the workforce those clients send to training — supervisors, operators, drivers, riggers, technicians, welders, electricians — and individual HSE officers researching the provider before a corporate booking.

## Product Purpose

QHSEMS Consultancy, Solution & Training LLP provides QHSE management-system consultancy, solutions and competency training so organisations build safer, healthier, compliant and sustainable workplaces. Success for the visitor is an enquiry: a scoped consultancy engagement, a training programme booked for a workforce, or a tender/pre-qualification conversation started.

Stated goal (verbatim from the company profile): "To develop competent HSE professionals, strengthen organizational safety culture, reduce workplace risks and support businesses in achieving sustainable HSE performance."

## Positioning

Practical over paper. The company's claim is that HSE performance is not achieved through documentation alone: it runs a seven-stage journey — Understand, Assess, Develop, Implement, Train & Develop, Monitor & Improve, Build HSE Culture — which means one provider owns both the management system AND the competence of the people who have to run it. Consultancies that only write manuals cannot deliver stage 5; training providers that only run courses cannot deliver stages 1-4. Thirty-plus years of that work sits in high-risk industries, not in a classroom.

## Operating Context

Delivery happens in the client's world, not ours: client sites, plants, projects and yards; instructor-led theory plus practical, demonstration, case studies, scenario-based exercises and competency assessment. Evaluation of this company typically runs through a tender/pre-qualification document, a company profile PDF, a training matrix, and a scope-of-work conversation. Real artefacts of the work: HSE Management System manuals, Project HSE Plans, Permit to Work systems, Job Safety Analyses, risk assessments, rescue plans, training matrices, KPI dashboards, audit and incident-investigation reports.

## Product Principles

1. **The catalogue is the proof.** Breadth and specificity of the ~110 named programmes demonstrate competence more credibly than any adjective. Make it navigable, never truncated into marketing.
2. **Systems and people are one offer.** Never present consultancy and training as two businesses; the seven-stage journey is the through-line that makes the company different.
3. **Speak to a buyer with a live problem.** Every route should let someone arrive at a hazard, a standard or a tender clause and reach the relevant capability in one or two moves.
4. **High-risk industry register.** Precision, restraint and legibility; the audience reads procedures for a living and distrusts decoration.
5. **Never invent credibility.** Real numbers, real catalogue, marked placeholders for everything unconfirmed.
