QHSEMS Consultancy, Solution & Training LLP is a professional QHSE consultancy, solutions and training organisation committed to helping businesses create safer, healthier, compliant and sustainable workplaces.

Who we are

We provide practical Health, Safety, Environment and Management Systems consultancy, training and competency-development solutions designed to meet industry requirements and recognised international standards.

With more than 30 years of experience across petrochemicals, oil & gas, heavy chemical and other manufacturing, construction, commissioning, energy, warehousing, healthcare and other high-risk industries, we deliver customised solutions that address real workplace challenges — not generic templates.

The work is deliberately unglamorous: writing the procedure that a supervisor will actually follow, running the drill until the response is automatic, and assessing the rigger until the lift plan is second nature.

How we work

Effective HSE performance is not achieved through documentation alone. We guide organisations through a structured, practical journey — from understanding risk to building a lasting safety culture.

  1. Understand

    Business activities, risks, workforce and legal requirements. Before anything is written, we walk the site and read the work as it is actually done.

  2. Assess

    Gap analyses, inspections and compliance reviews against legislation, client requirements and international standards.

  3. Develop

    Practical QHSE management systems and documentation — policies, manuals, procedures, project HSE plans and training matrices.

  4. Implement

    Leadership engagement and operational controls, so the system is owned by the line rather than filed by the HSE department.

  5. Train & Develop

    Competency development and safe working behaviour, delivered to the people who carry the risk.

  6. Monitor & Improve

    Audits, KPI tracking and management review, with corrective and preventive action managed through to closure.

  7. Build HSE Culture

    A proactive culture beyond compliance — where hazards are reported because people want to, not because they have to.
