Your Industry. Your Risks. Our Expertise.

Every industry has different risks, requirements and working conditions. We understand your challenges and provide practical QHSE solutions designed around your business — anywhere in India.

Industries We Support

  • Oil & Gas

    Permit to work, H2S awareness and SCBA, confined space entry, gas testing and emergency response for upstream and downstream operations.

  • Petrochemicals

    Process-plant HSE management systems, hot work, LOTO, HAZMAT handling and fire watch for continuous operations.

  • Manufacturing

    Machine and electrical safety, LOTO, chemical handling, ergonomics and safety culture for heavy chemical and other manufacturing.

  • Construction

    Project HSE plans, contractor HSE management, work at height, scaffolding, lifting and rigging, excavation and trench safety.

  • Energy

    Electrical and intrinsic safety, permit to work, LOTO and emergency preparedness for power and energy sites.

  • Warehousing & Logistics

    Forklift safe operation and competency, manual handling, fire safety and defensive driving for warehouses and fleets.

  • Healthcare

    Fire safety and evacuation, first aid and CPR, chemical and laboratory safety, and staff wellbeing programmes.

  • Commissioning & Projects

    Project HSE plans, permit to work, contractor coordination, emergency planning and competency assurance for project teams.

We also support other industries based on their specific QHSE requirements.
