How a consultancy engagement runs

The same seven stages every time, scaled to the size of the problem. A tender response might take two of them; a greenfield management system takes all seven.

See the full approach →

A system nobody can follow on a night shift is not a system. It is a document.

  1. Understand

    Business activities, risks, workforce and legal requirements.

  2. Assess

    Gap analyses, inspections and compliance reviews.

  3. Develop

    Practical QHSE management systems and documentation.

  4. Implement

    Leadership engagement and operational controls.

  5. Train & Develop

    Competency development and safe working behaviour.

  6. Monitor & Improve

    Audits, KPI tracking and management review.

  7. Build HSE Culture

    A proactive culture beyond compliance.
